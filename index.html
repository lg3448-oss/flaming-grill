Our Story

Flaming Grill began in 2015 with one simple idea: great ingredients deserve a little fire and a lot of care.

Our chefs bring together grilling traditions from Japan, Korea, and beyond. Every cut, vegetable, and sauce is selected to make the meal feel effortless and memorable.

Today, our Brick location welcomes guests who come for the food, stay for the atmosphere, and return for the warmth of the flame.
